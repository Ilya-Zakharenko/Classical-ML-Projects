# <center> **PROJECT: Bank Client Segmentation**

Unsupervised learning project for customer segmentation of credit card holders to enable personalized marketing campaigns.

---

### **Project Goal**

Perform customer segmentation based on credit card usage behavior over the last 6 months. The resulting segments will help the bank's marketing team create more targeted and personalized promotional offers.

---

### **Business Problem**

A New York bank has data on **8,950** credit card holders. To increase the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, it is necessary to divide clients into homogeneous groups (segments) according to their behavior and financial activity.

---

### **Dataset**

- **Number of observations**: 8,950 clients
- **Time period**: Last 6 months of credit card usage
- **Features**: Demographic information, transaction behavior, spending patterns, credit limit usage, payment habits, etc.

---

### **Technologies Used**

- `pandas`, `numpy`
- `scikit-learn` (for clustering and preprocessing)
- `matplotlib`, `seaborn` (for visualization)
- `sklearn.cluster` (KMeans, etc.)

---

### **Project Stages**

1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Feature scaling and engineering
4. Determining optimal number of clusters (Elbow Method, Silhouette Score)
5. Clustering model training (K-Means and others)
6. Cluster interpretation and profiling
7. Business recommendations for each segment

---

### **Key Achievements**

- Successfully segmented 8,950 credit card clients into meaningful groups
- Identified distinct customer profiles (e.g., high spenders, low activity, premium users, etc.)
- Provided actionable insights for personalized marketing strategies
- Applied best practices in unsupervised learning and customer segmentation

---

### **Conclusion**

This project demonstrates strong skills in customer segmentation using unsupervised learning. The developed clusters allow the bank to move from mass marketing to personalized offers, which should significantly increase the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and customer loyalty.
