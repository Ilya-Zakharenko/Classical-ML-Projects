# <center> **Clustering Tasks**

This folder contains projects focused on **unsupervised learning** — specifically clustering and customer segmentation tasks. These projects demonstrate the ability to discover hidden patterns and group similar data points without labeled targets.

---

### 📁 Projects

- **[Bank Client Segmentation](Bank-Client-Segmentation/)**  
  Segmentation of credit card holders to enable more personalized marketing campaigns.

- **[Customer Segmentation with RFM](Customer-Segmentation-with-RFM/)**  
  RFM-based clustering of online store customers to identify loyal, promising, new, and churned segments and develop targeted retention strategies.

- **[Physical Activity Data Clustering](Physical-Activity-Data-Clustering/)**  
  Clustering of sensor data from wearable devices (IMU + heart rate) to automatically recognize different types of physical activity.

- **[Scintillation Detector Signal Clustering](Scintillation-Detector/)**  
  Unsupervised clustering of signals from a scintillation detector to separate gamma quanta, neutrons, and anomalous signals in nuclear physics experiments.

### 🛠 Technologies & Approaches

**Core Libraries**
- `pandas`, `numpy`
- `scikit-learn`
- `matplotlib`, `seaborn`, `plotly`

**Clustering Algorithms**
- K-Means
- Hierarchical Clustering
- DBSCAN
- Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM)

**Techniques**
- RFM analysis
- Feature scaling and engineering
- Dimensionality reduction (PCA, t-SNE)
- Cluster interpretation and profiling
- Elbow method, Silhouette score for optimal cluster selection
